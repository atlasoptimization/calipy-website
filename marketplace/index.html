A planned marketplace
for models and effects.

A planned place to discover, buy, and sell scientific models and reusable effects. So the next team can start with an explanation, not an empty file.

Register marketplace interest →

The marketplace idea

Discover and evaluate
reusable components.

Imagine evaluating a calibration model by its assumptions, interface, evidence, and license before bringing it into your own analysis. Or publishing an effect you have developed so others can build on it.

The marketplace is a product concept. There are no live listings, purchases, seller accounts, or marketplace downloads today.

ILLUSTRATIVE LISTING STRUCTURE

A scientific component,
with its context.

Purpose
What question does it answer?
Interface
Inputs, outputs, dimensions, constraints
Evidence
Examples, validation, assumptions, limits
Compatibility
Required CaliPy version and dependencies
Terms
Author, usage license, price, support

A proposed listing format, not an item for sale.

For creators and users

For model creators and users.

DISCOVER

Start from relevant work.

The goal is to find components by scientific purpose and inspect the example and assumptions before choosing one.

BUILD

Bring your expertise.

Creators could offer reusable effects or complete models, with clear licenses and evidence supporting the intended use.

REVIEW

Keep compatibility visible.

Version requirements, scientific interfaces, provenance, and limitations would help users assess whether a component belongs in their workflow.

Tell us what you would share or use.

Tell us what you would like to reuse or offer: the instrument, the modeling problem, and the evidence someone would need to trust it. Seller terms, review rules, payments, pricing, and release timing are still to be defined.
