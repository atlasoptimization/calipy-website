About CaliPy

About CaliPy
and Atlas Optimization.

CaliPy brings a scientific explanation into the analysis: what generated the readings, what is uncertain, and what the evidence can support.

From measurement science

Why CaliPy
was developed.

CaliPy stands for Calibration Library for Python. It is developed by Dr. Jemil Avers Butt at Atlas Optimization GmbH, growing out of work on probabilistic instrument models, calibration, and scientific uncertainty.

The aim is to help researchers and engineers turn knowledge of an experiment into a model they can run, inspect, and improve. PyTorch supplies tensor computation and differentiation; Pyro supplies probabilistic inference.

What guides the work

Principles behind the library.

01 / EXPLANATION

Start with how data happen.

Physical relationships, instrument behavior, and variation should have explicit roles in the model.

02 / REUSE

Let useful work travel.

Build scientific effects with meaningful interfaces so the next model can build on work already done.

03 / EVIDENCE

Keep the result accountable.

Retain assumptions, diagnostics, and validation limits alongside an estimate or prediction.

Where we are

Current availability
and development.

CaliPy is pre-alpha. The public Python release is available; the newer block API, visual workbench, and optional local agent bridge are in development. Commercial editions and the marketplace are being shaped with prospective users.
