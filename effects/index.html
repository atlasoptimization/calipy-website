Inside CaliPy / composable effects

How CaliPy
effects compose.

Describe a measurement as a chain of meaningful parts. Reuse those parts when the experiment changes, while keeping the assumptions open to inspection.

A measurement has a story

Quantities, effects, and observations.

Conceptual example: a sensor measures a changing physical quantity through an imperfect response.

  1. 01 / QUANTITYPhysical signal

    The level you want to learn about.

  2. 02 / RESPONSESensor behavior

    How the instrument transforms it.

  3. 03 / VARIATIONMeasurement law

    How readings vary around that response.

  4. 04 / EVIDENCEObserved readings

    The data connected to the model.

Small parts, explicit meaning.

A quantity can be known, optimized as a point, or represented as a prior-backed unknown. An effect transforms or generates quantities. An observation attaches measured data to a stochastic output.

A model you can inspect together.

In the development interface, effects describe their inputs, outputs, dimensions, and constraints. Python, the workbench, and the optional agent bridge use these shared scientific contracts.

Reuse with a reason

Replacing an effect:
what needs to agree?

Starting hypothesis

Constant measurement noise

Use one noise scale across all readings when that assumption matches the experiment.

→
Alternative to investigate

Signal-dependent noise

Investigate a scale that changes with the response when residuals suggest changing variation.

This is a change in the scientific explanation, not just a different picture. The surrounding model may be reusable, but the alternative needs compatible connections, suitable parameters, a new fit, and its own validation.

01 / INSPECT

Know what connects.

Inputs, outputs, named dimensions, and constraints describe the interface. Shared parameter identity and prior meaning also matter.

02 / PREVIEW

Review the change.

Development authoring checks replacements and reports compatibility, issues needing review, or incompatibility. A similar shape alone is insufficient.

03 / EVALUATE

Ask whether it explains more.

Compare assumptions, diagnostics, and predictions against appropriate evidence. A compatible replacement is not a guarantee of a better model.

Conceptual walkthrough of the development workflow. This page does not execute a replacement, and arbitrary effects are not interchangeable.

Why this matters

What composition
helps you reuse.

For a scientist, reuse means a tested idea can become part of the next experiment. For a team, an explicit model makes handoff and review easier to structure. For an agent, described operations provide context for a proposal that a person can inspect.
